# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a technical community where people can participate without harassment or
intimidation. Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- communicate with respect and assume good faith while evaluating evidence
  critically;
- welcome questions and different levels of experience;
- focus review on behavior, risks, and maintainability rather than the person;
- protect private information and follow coordinated disclosure for security
  reports;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, and
  publication of another person's private information.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests,
reviews, discussions, and project-related public events or communication.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other material that
violates this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented
privately when appropriate, and avoid exposing reporters or affected people.

If a private project conduct channel is published, use it. Until then, report
platform-level abuse through GitHub's private reporting controls and contact a
maintainer privately through their verified GitHub profile. Do not disclose
sensitive conduct reports in a public issue.

Maintainers must not promise confidentiality they cannot technically provide.
Reports made through third-party platforms are also subject to those platforms'
data handling.
